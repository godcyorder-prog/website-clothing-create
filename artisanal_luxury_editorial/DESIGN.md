## Brand & Style
This design system embodies the understated elegance of modern artisanal Indian couture. It merges traditional craftsmanship—handlooms, delicate chanderi, fine silks, and zari weaves—with clean, high-fashion minimalism. The interface serves as a quiet gallery frame that recedes, allowing rich textile photography, drape, and texture to command attention.

The aesthetic evokes serene luxury, intentional pacing, and tactile heritage. It targets discerning shoppers seeking timeless slow fashion over transient trends. Visual density is deliberately low, relying on generous whitespace, strict typographic harmony, hairline framing, and absolute restraint in ornamentation.

## Colors
The palette is rooted in pure minimalism with organic warmth:
- **Canvas & Surfaces**: Crisp white (`#FFFFFF`) serves as the foundational primary canvas, while warm alabaster (`#F8F7F4`) provides subtle sectional shifts for editorial callouts, product detail trays, and filters.
- **Text & Hierarchy**: Primary typography and high-emphasis elements use `#1A1A1A` for soft charcoal contrast rather than harsh pitch black. Secondary descriptions use `#666666`, while muted metadata and breadcrumbs rest at `#8A8A8A`.
- **Architectural Framing**: Hairline borders strictly utilize `#E5E5E5` to articulate product grids and dividers without creating visual heaviness.
- **Accents**: Deep crimson (`#B42318`) is reserved exclusively for editorial sales, waitlists, and urgent alerts. Forest sage (`#3F6B4A`) signals positive states, sustainable craft certifications, and in-stock inventory indicators.

## Typography
Typographic rhythm balances high-fashion editorial serifs with functional precision:
- **Headings (Playfair Display)**: Rendered in light and regular weights with generous breathing room. For hero features, italicized editorial phrases provide a boutique look.
- **Interface & Body (Inter)**: Set with lightened weights (`300` and `400`) to evoke airiness. 
- **Micro-copy & Navigation (Inter Uppercase)**: Navigation categories, badge tags, product specifications, and action labels utilize tracked uppercase lettering (`letterSpacing: 0.14em` to `0.18em`) for quiet architectural structure.
- **Currency & Pricing**: Rendered in clean sans-serif with tabular numerals to ensure scannability across size matrices and cart drawers.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a fluid-yet-disciplined 12-column grid on desktop, 8 columns on tablet, and 4 columns on mobile. 

- **Outer Margins**: Desktop margins expand up to `5rem` (`80px`), creating an expansive frame around lookbooks and product carousels. Mobile layouts compress margins to `1.25rem` to maximize imagery width.
- **Rhythm**: Editorial sections use generous vertical spacing (`space-xl` and `space-2xl`) to avoid a cluttered catalog appearance. Product listings favor an asymmetrical editorial flow, alternating between 2-column large portraits and 3-column product grids.
- **Aspect Ratios**: Imagery adheres to strict vertical ratios (`3:4` or `4:5`) honoring full-length traditional silhouettes and textile draping.

## Elevation & Depth
Depth is created through structural layering, white space, and hairline borders rather than traditional drop shadows:
- **Flat Architectural Planar Surface**: Surfaces sit completely flat. Standard drop shadows are eliminated across cards, buttons, and panels.
- **Hairlines**: Division between sections, headers, and footer components is managed through `1px solid #E5E5E5`.
- **Modals & Drawers**: Cart slides and quick-view overlays use a subtle warm backdrop overlay (`rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.35)`) combined with an ultra-soft, diffused shadow (`0 20px 40px -15px rgba(26, 26, 26, 0.08)`) to maintain an airy aesthetic.
- **Hover Transitions**: Interactions avoid physical lift. Instead, hover triggers smooth opacity changes, image crossfades, or clean border outlines.

## Shapes
In alignment with couture editorial standards, the shape language uses pure rectangular geometry (`roundedness: 0`). 

All buttons, inputs, badge tags, image frames, and floating drawers maintain crisp 90-degree corners. This evokes the razor-sharp tailoring of bespoke garments, architectural gallery spaces, and fine paper stock. Subtle exceptions are limited to circular swatches representing fabric textures and color palettes.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary**: Solid `#1A1A1A` fill with `#FFFFFF` text. Sharp corners, height `48px`, horizontal padding `2rem`. Typography is uppercase Inter with `letter-spacing: 0.14em`. Hover state transitions to `#333333` with immediate, fluid responsiveness.
- **Secondary / Outline**: 1px solid border `#1A1A1A` with transparent background and `#1A1A1A` text. Hover shifts background to `#F8F7F4`.
- **Text Link**: Understated `#1A1A1A` text with a permanent or animated 1px hairline underline offset by 4px.

### Product Cards
- Flat presentation with vertical `3:4` imagery.
- Hairline `1px` border appears only on hover or sits quietly as a separator grid.
- Metadata is center-aligned or left-aligned with a high-fashion aesthetic: Product Title in `Playfair Display` (16px), Fabric/Technique subline in `Inter` muted (12px), and Price in clean sans-serif (14px).
- Secondary image hover reveals artisan details or movement draping.

### Chips & Filter Swatches
- Minimalist text boxes framed in `1px solid #E5E5E5`. Selected state transitions to solid `#1A1A1A` with inverted white text.
- Fabric and color filters utilize 24px circular swatches framed by a fine hairline ring when active.

### Form Inputs & Fields
- Bottom-border only (`1px solid #E5E5E5`) for high-end boutique minimalism, or fully framed hairline boxes for complex checkouts.
- Focus state switches the border to `#1A1A1A`. 
- Placeholder text in `#8A8A8A` set in light typography that transitions to a floating uppercase label.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- Custom square selectors with zero border radius. Active state fills with `#1A1A1A` and displays an understated white check or inner square.

### Editorial Story Callouts
- Alternating panels featuring `#F8F7F4` backgrounds with rich artisanal narratives, quote glyphs, weaver provenance details, and curated looks.
